# Room opaque transmission — v1

This documents the original opaque component. See `Opening-transmission-method.md` for the current v2 extension and backward compatibility.

Source basis: the envelope heat-transfer relationship Q = U × A × ΔT explicitly retained in `Requirements/Mechanical-Load-Skill-Creation.md`; assembly U follows the sourced method in `Envelope-assembly-method.md`. This implementation adds a room/surface ledger to the existing elementary conduction arithmetic, not a full ACCA calculation.

IP contract: source-backed room temperature and per-surface coincident adjacent temperature in °F; gross and named opening areas in ft²; U recomputed from the referenced assembly in Btuh/(ft²·°F). Net opaque area = gross − sum(openings). Positive net area is required. Q = U × Anet × (Troom − Tadjacent), positive outward. Preserve separate outward losses and inward gains and their signed balance. No automatic gain credit for equipment sizing. All scalar sources/classifications, room author/source/date and the full surface/opening ledger are retained. No defaults for geometry, setpoints or weather are supplied.

Supported scope is above-grade opaque steady-state surfaces. No transient cooling/solar, ground coupling, fenestration, ventilation/infiltration, distribution or complete load calculation. Opening deductions remove area from the opaque subtotal; they do not assign zero load to windows/doors. Exported room reviews list every excluded category and include the referenced assembly inputs/results. Assembly references are live, not frozen snapshots; recalculation and existing whole-project QA fingerprints reflect changed assembly inputs. Invalid and missing references fail portable/native validation. Future room revision/history and source-geometry derivation remain separate work.

Verification: RoomTransmissionTests covers known area deductions and signed balance (U0.0875; exterior 200−30−20 ft² at ΔT60 gives787.5 Btuh outward, partition100 ft² at ΔT−10 gives87.5 Btuh inward, net700); full native package roundtrip and unknown provenance preservation; assembly resistance doubling halves loads and invalidates prior QA; invalid geometry, unresolved evidence, absolute-zero/nonfinite temperatures, missing references, duplicate names/IDs, strict request fields, QA reopening and atomic failures. The installed plugin workflow performs room.transmission.create and room-review against a temporary synthetic fixture. These values are analytical tests, not project design inputs or field validation.
