# Whole-product opening transmission — room method v2

Primary source context: [NFRC residential certification](https://nfrc.org/residential/) identifies whole product performance ratings for windows/doors, and [DOE whole-product door U-factor guidance](https://www.energycodes.gov/technical-assistance/faqs/when-entering-doors-comcheck-do-i-use-operable-u-factor-or-caclulated-u) explains inclusion of the frame rather than isolated glass/slab performance. The app does not determine certification, infer a product rating or claim adopted-code compliance from these sources.

The existing room conduction equation applies separately to each opening: Qoutward = Uwhole product × Afull product × (Troom − Tadjacent). U uses Btuh/(ft²·°F), A uses ft², temperatures use °F and Q uses Btuh. Product configuration, rating source and classification travel with each U input. Rated-condition applicability remains an engineering review responsibility. No solar, leakage, additional film resistance or rating-to-design correction is automatically added.

Areas are deducted once from gross opaque area and added once in opening transmission. Existing opaque result fields retain their meaning. Opening results are separate, signed and traceable. Known opening loss/gain subtotals include only resolved inputs. Optional combined envelope loss/gain/net fields are available only when every listed opening has valid U; missing input stays unknown even at zero ΔT. An empty opening list needs no opening ratings, but does not independently verify the drawing has no openings. Combined subtotals cover only the entered ledger, not complete room geometry or all load components.

New saved records identify v2. v1 records remain valid only without U inputs, so earlier calculations are not silently reinterpreted under their original method label. Legacy serialized results without the optional openingTransmission field decode successfully. Native controls expose rating availability, full product area/U/source, missing data and equation traces. Surfaces still require positive net opaque area; fully glazed surfaces, separate opening-only surfaces, room revisions and complete load methods remain outstanding.

Verification: six OpeningTransmissionTests cover analytical closure without double-counting (opaque787.5 + openings1140 =1927.5 outward Btuh, inward87.5, net1840), mixed known/missing ratings, zero ΔT unknown propagation, inward flow, no-opening cases, nonpositive/nonfinite/overflow inputs, unresolved/blank evidence, native package roundtrip, strict schema and legacy record/result compatibility. The installed plugin fixture saves unrated and rated cases, reviews their contrasting completeness states, preserves originals and rejects overwrite. Synthetic U-values are test inputs only.
