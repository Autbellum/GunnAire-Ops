# Sourced schedule unit conventions — 2026-09-10

## Implemented scope

A schedule column can retain its literal ambiguous unit and separately record one of five supported conventions with a source citation. Missing evidence, mismatched units/fields and unknown nested contract fields reject the map. Source changes affect row identity; before/after map history retains the convention. Package and portable JSON preserve the source, literal cells and unknowns. Native editing exposes the citation and resets it when field/unit/source changes.

Supported conventions are International Table Btu/h, thousands of Btu IT/h, refrigeration tons, US liquid GPM and Imperial GPM. Conversion relationships and the requirement for actual legend/specification evidence are documented in [schedule numeric units](../engineering/Schedule-numeric-units.md). Source citations are recorded input, not authenticated or independently machine-verified evidence.
