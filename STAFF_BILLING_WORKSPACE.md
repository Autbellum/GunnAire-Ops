# Independent staff billing workspace — 2026-09-11

## Actual gap and delivered path

The owner app already has full invoice/estimate builders, catalog publication and a server-mediated QBO billing workflow. An independent CloudKit staff login does not use that owner ModelContainer: it opens the separate authorized projection workspace. Before this change its invoice/estimate routes used a generic scalar-field list, omitted saved sold lines and exposed internal identifiers/signature encodings as ordinary fields. A row could be titled merely `unpaid`. Those paths were not equivalent to complete staff invoicing.

The independent staff route now opens a typed invoice/estimate document inside the existing per-window navigation stack. The list identifies customer, work type, saved amount, status and creation date, with search. Search survives verified snapshot refresh and clears on account loss or a destination change. The document presents the saved service/repair/replacement type, original sold names, descriptions, quantities, rates, extended amounts, approved document discount, saved tax and total. Optional package contents, included assembly parts, equipment context, notes, approval and related records are disclosed progressively. Original sold prices never come from the current pricebook.

Customer, job, service-location, current-catalog and equipment links use compound record routes inside the same authorized projection. Related files, message records and payment records match the exact invoice/estimate identity, not every record sharing its customer/job. Missing, restricted or ambiguous links never open the owner store or an arbitrary record. These are record handoffs, not new attachment downloading, live mail composition or payment collection.

The typed view does not carry private item costs, QBO IDs, provider scope, raw signature bytes, JSON or protocol metadata. A missing legacy itemization is explicitly distinguished from an empty/zero itemized invoice. Legacy fractional-cent amounts are displayed without rounding, with office-review guidance. Itemized financial evidence remains exact-cent reconciled. Tax states remain explicit; absent tax evidence is not inferred from a restricted QBO ID. The last QBO balance is an observation, not a collectible balance, and visible payment rows never recalculate it.

Live loading validates the hosted journal and compares activated records with the immutable accepted plan. Tampered, duplicate, absent, mismatched-kind and internally inconsistent documents fail closed. This adds no role grant, owner-model reconstruction, model schema, transport route, financial mutation or CloudKit capability.
