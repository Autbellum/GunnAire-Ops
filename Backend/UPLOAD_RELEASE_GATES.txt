Backend-only versus native release
Physical-device testing is NOT intrinsically required to deploy this additive
backend contract separately: legacy POST remains supported, native source/build
is unchanged, and the actual native recovery decoder has synthetic wire evidence.
That conclusion is scoped to backend release, not a claim that installed native
apps or device offline/relaunch flows were exercised. Require backend migration,
resource/contract/security evidence, approved release configuration, writer drain,
backup continuity and a controlled backend acceptance/rollback plan before release.
A new native distribution or claim of end-to-end device recovery still requires
signed iPad/Mac/device acceptance, CloudKit/APNs configuration and relevant network
loss/relaunch/file-retention checks under the separate native release plan.

Remaining release actions: review exact successor CI artifact; fresh volume and
backup-staging sizing; choose/apply approved policy; configure alerts/upload pause;
drain old writers; approve backend-only deployment and perform controlled actor,
collision/replay/hash/low-space acceptance. No deployment authority is conveyed by
synthetic branch/Actions approval. Keep v2/new-only keys. Any rollback to040/aa
requires uploads paused and additive tables/files/client intents retained. Never
restore an older DB over new writes or revert to single-key code.
