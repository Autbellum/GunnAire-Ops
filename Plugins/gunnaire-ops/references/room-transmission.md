# Room transmission, including rated openings

`room.transmission.create` is an apply operation. Required top-level fields: operation, author, name, source, indoorDesignF, surfaces. Its receipt returns a new recordID. Use `scripts/loadsight.py room-review project.json` for recalculated results and supporting assembly records. The wrapper invokes the same Swift SDK as the native app and refuses existing output paths.

A sourced scalar is exactly `{"value":70,"source":"Specific design-case reference","classification":"USER-PROVIDED"}`. Allowed classifications are EXTRACTED, USER-PROVIDED, CODE-DEFAULT, ENGINEERING-ASSUMPTION and RFI-REQUIRED; unresolved RFI values cannot produce saved calculations. Numeric inputs must be finite, sources nonblank.

indoorDesignF is a sourced scalar in °F. Each surface requires exactly name, assemblyID, grossAreaSF, openings, adjacentDesignF. grossAreaSF is a positive sourced ft² value INCLUDING its openings; adjacentDesignF is the sourced coincident temperature on the other side of that surface in °F. assemblyID must identify an existing saved envelope assembly. Ground/slab surfaces are outside this method. Surface names must be distinct within a room.

openings is a required array. Every opening requires name and areaSF (a positive sourced ft² scalar), with optional wholeProductU (the same sourced scalar shape, positive Btuh/(ft²·°F)). wholeProductU may be omitted or null when unknown; unknown keys are rejected. Use full product area and U including the frame/complete door; cite product configuration and rating basis. Center-of-glass or slab-only values are not whole-product ratings. Opening names must be distinct within the parent surface; deductions must leave positive net opaque area. An explicit empty array declares no openings. The native app requires an explicit no-openings toggle when the list is empty. Neither entered areas nor their sources establish independent drawing verification.

Equation: Anet = Agross − ΣAopening; Qoutward = Uassembly × Anet × (Troom − Tadjacent). U is recalculated from the saved assembly each time. Per-surface opaque results include area, U, ΔT, signed Q and traces. The openingTransmission result separately reports each opening Q, known opening loss/gain, allListedOpeningsRated and combinedEnvelopeLossBtuh / combinedEnvelopeGainBtuh / combinedEnvelopeNetOutwardBtuh. Combined fields are absent/unknown if any opening lacks U, even at zero ΔT. Known-only subtotals never establish complete envelope coverage. Room results separately report outwardLossBtuh, inwardGainBtuh and netOutwardBtuh = loss − gain. Negative surface Q is inward gain; it is not silently clamped or granted an equipment-sizing credit. All temperatures must belong to the same documented design case.

Room records append with identity/date, full inputs, method and assumptions, reopening QA. Unknown fields on existing records are preserved. Changing an assembly changes the recomputed linked room result and invalidates fingerprinted QA. There is no historical assembly snapshot in a room record; it references the current saved assembly by ID. Invalid/missing references and duplicate IDs fail native/portable validation.

This is a **partial room transmission worksheet**, not a complete heating/cooling load, Manual J/N implementation or equipment selection. Opening conduction is calculated when U is supplied; missing opening ratings remain unknown. Ground coupling, infiltration/ventilation, duct/distribution, solar/internal gains and transient effects are unmodeled, not zero. The rated-input status does not assert independent product certification or verification of the entered geometry. Room results enumerate excluded components. Complete room/zone/block loads and drawing-derived areas remain outstanding. Native room revisions and their input history are now implemented.

New records use `Room opaque and opening steady-state transmission; IP v2`. Legacy v1 records without U remain valid and receive unknown opening results. Attaching new U inputs to a v1 record is rejected; create a new v2 design case instead. Whole-product ratings are sourced input values, not a built-in certification lookup, product recommendation or automatic conversion of rating conditions to actual design films. No extra surface-film resistance is applied.


## Revise an existing room case

Read `room-review` first. Each room entry includes `editFingerprint`. `room.transmission.revise` requires the same author/name/source/indoorDesignF/surfaces payload as create, plus id, expectedFingerprint and reason. Copy the exact current editFingerprint into expectedFingerprint; never invent it or silently retry a rejected revision with a newly fetched token. Review/reload changed inputs first. The token covers full room inputs, its latest revision identity and the available assembly catalog (including unknown source extensions). Assembly additions or edits invalidate it; unrelated room/QA edits do not.

A successful revision preserves room ID and room count, replaces the explicitly supplied inputs using method v2, retains unknown top-level room extensions, and reopens QA. Nested surface/opening arrays are replacement inputs; their previous complete raw values, including extensions, remain in the before snapshot. Existing other room records/history remain unchanged. Source and revision author are required; the native form clears the old author's name when loading a case. Native controls confirm before discarding a populated unsaved draft.

room-review includes history entries with id, roomID, author, recordedAt, reason, before, after and assemblyBasis, plus recomputed beforeResult and afterResult with equation traces. Derived results are not trusted from stored history fields. Basis snapshots include assemblies referenced by either before or after inputs, so both can be recomputed at the revision's saved basis. They do not reconstruct an unrecorded earlier assembly state predating the revision. History chain continuity and latest-room consistency are validated. The local JSON can be edited outside the app: these checks are not cryptographic attestation, authenticated identity or release approval. Unknown/missing tokens, invalid input, broken histories and stale edits fail atomically; apply still writes only to a new path.
